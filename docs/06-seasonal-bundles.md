# 06 · Seasonal Bundles

A bundle is three or four of your existing products, one new "cover and how
to use this" page, and a better price. It is the highest-leverage hour of
product work you will do in any month, because it sells things you already
built to buyers who need less convincing. This chapter gives the calendar
and the math.

## The year, through your products' eyes

Digital-download demand has tides. Aim every bundle at one tide:

| Season | What rises | Bundle to have ready |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dec–Jan | New-year planners, budgeting, habit restarts | Budget sheet + habit tracker + paperwork starter kit |
| Mar–May | Spring cleaning — paperwork especially | Household binder + organizer refill pack |
| Jun–Sep | Teacher prep and "Q4 is coming" resets | Classroom organizer set, shift-printables refresh |
| Oct–Dec | Gifts, cozy projects, tax-year wrap-up | Winter planning kit + gift-budget sheet + prompt pack |

Two honest notes before the calendar looks easy:

- **Don't bundle things built for one moment.** A "2026 dated planner" is
  worthless on January 1. Dated products need annual refreshes — doable in an
  afternoon if the dates live on their own pages (see below), punishing if
  they don't.
- **Gift-season no-guilt rule:** never pad a bundle with $1.99 fillers just
  to inflate the "worth" claim. Buyers notice, and it teaches them not to
  trust your real prices.

## Bundle math

Round-number example from the product families in
[Chapter 1](01-what-to-make.html):

| Item | Standalone price |
| --- | --- |
| AI prompt worksheet pack | $12 |
| Care-shift organizer set | $6 |
| Household paperwork binder | $15 |
| **If bought separately** | **$33** |
| Typical bundle price (60–70% of the sum) | **$19–$23** |

Two rules keep that table honest:

1. **Bundle price sits at 60–70% of the sum of standalone prices.** High
   enough to respect the singles, low enough to feel like a real discount.
2. **Show the comparison in the listing.** A preview slide or bullet with
   "$33 if bought separately" does the arithmetic for the buyer. Honesty is
   the feature.

Fee note: bundles also fix the flat-fee problem from
[Chapter 2](02-platforms-and-fee-math.html) — a $22 sale loses the same
$0.25–$0.30 flat fee that a $6 sale loses, so the effective fee rate drops
almost a full percentage point per bundle.

## Refresh instead of retire

The seasonal bump is the *best* time to do maintenance on products that
already sell:

- **Dated products:** keep every calendar-specific page on its own PDF pages
  (say pages 3–5). Then "2027 edition" is replacing three pages and bumping
  the version footer — an afternoon, not a rebuild.
- **Version footers:** bump it whenever you change files (see
  [Chapter 5](05-copy-theft-response.html) for why visible versions also
  make theft obvious).
- **One new preview image per refresh:** a season-dressed mockup — an
  October cover photo, spring colors. Same product, new shelf appeal.
- **Retire gracefully:** when a product is superseded, mark the old listing
  "2026 edition — last chance" for one season, then let it rest. Retired
  products that still sell are fine; leave them be.

## The yearly rhythm

Three reliable bundle slots, each needing at most an afternoon a week of
prep:

1. **January reset** — budget/habit bundle; launch late December.
2. **Back-to-school / Q4 prep** — organization bundle; launch August.
3. **Gift season** — the "whole family" bundle; launch early November. Early
   matters: marketplace search rewards arriving before the rush, not during
   it.

Pair each slot with one email to your list, and — when the
*publishing-beyond-kdp-guide* promo calendar is running — stagger
them: two emails about two different products outperform three emails about
one product.

## Where bundles sell best

- **Etsy:** bundles work and standalone buyers are already there. Make the
  "what's included" slide map each page to its origin product — bundle
  returns come from confusion, not stinginess.
- **Gumroad/Ko-fi:** the storefront is simple, the fee math favors single
  larger transactions, and bundle copy ("the whole kit") reads naturally.
- **Your own site and list:** the cleanest experience of all — but only ship
  it when [Chapter 7](07-when-to-leave-marketplaces.html) conditions are
  true.
