# 02 · Platforms and Fee Math, in Round Numbers

Three storefronts cover almost everything a small digital shop needs: **Etsy**
(buyers come to you, fees are theirs), **Gumroad** and **Ko-fi** (you bring the
buyers, fees are smaller). This chapter does the arithmetic with round numbers
so you can decide without opening a spreadsheet.

> Rates below were true at time of writing and are rounded for clarity.
> Before pricing anything, check each platform's current fee page — these
> change every year or two.

## The three, in one breath

| | **Etsy** | **Gumroad** | **Ko-fi** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who finds you | Their search + ads bring buyers | You bring buyers (via links) | You bring buyers (via links) |
| Listing fee | $0.20 per listing | $0 | $0 |
| Platform cut | About 6.5% | About 10% | About 5% (0% with Gold, ~$7/mo) |
| Payment processing | About 3% + $0.25 | Included in the 10% + $0.30 | About 3% + $0.25 (direct to you) |
| Payout rhythm | Deposits daily/weekly | Weekly | Instant-ish, processor direct |
| Best at | Cold traffic, printables discovery | Higher-priced packs with an audience | Tipping-café vibe, community first |

## One transaction, three receipts

Say you sell a **$10** printable. Roughly:

- **Etsy:** $0.20 listing + 6.5% ($0.65) + ~3% + $0.25 processing ($0.55)
  ≈ **$1.40 in fees → you keep about $8.60** (~14% given back)
- **Gumroad:** 10% + $0.30 ≈ **$1.30 → you keep about $8.70** (~13%)
- **Ko-fi (free plan):** 5% ($0.50) + ~3% + $0.25 ($0.55) ≈ **$1.05 → you keep
  about $8.95** (~10%)

Now a **$25** planning kit:

- **Etsy:** $0.20 + $1.63 + $1.00 ≈ **$2.85 → keep about $22.15** (~11%)
- **Gumroad:** 10% + $0.30 = **$2.80 → keep about $22.20** (~11%)
- **Ko-fi:** 5% ($1.25) + $1.00 ≈ **$2.25 → keep about $22.75** (~9%)

**Round-number law:** flat fees bite small orders hardest, percentage fees bite
every order equally. Under $10, the three platforms converge; above $25, Ko-fi
pulls ahead strictly on fees.

## A month of steady selling

Suppose you sell $500 in a month (e.g. 50 × $10, a realistic early-stage
printables side):

| Platform | Fees that month (approx.) | You keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Etsy | ~$72 (14%) | ~$428 |
| Gumroad | ~$65 (13%) | ~$435 |
| Ko-fi free plan | ~$55 (10%) | ~$445 |

At $500 a month, **the fee gap is about $20 — a rounding error.** The choice
between platforms is almost never about fees at small scale; it is about
traffic (Etsy has it) and control (Ko-fi/Gumroad give it to you).

## The hidden costs fees don't show

- **Etsy:** you are renting search position. Fees can rise (they have before),
  and policy mistakes can suspend a shop with your best asset inside it.
- **Gumroad:** their ~10% covers hosting, checkout, email receipt, and taxes
  paperwork — it *is* a storefront you don't maintain. If a sale arrives from
  Gumroad Discover, their share rises to around 30%, so don't count on
  Discover sellers in your budget.
- **Ko-fi:** the shop is bare-bones. 0%-fee Gold membership (~$7/mo, check their
  current pricing page) only pays off above roughly **$150/mo in shop
  sales**; below that the free plan's 5% is cheaper than the subscription.

## Pricing implications in one minute

1. **Skip the $9.99 tricks; price in whole dollars.** Round pricing reads as
   friendlier on Ko-fi/Gumroad where checkout feels personal; dense
   marketplace search cards tolerate charmless numbers better anyway.
2. **Bundle smalls.** Alone, a $3 printable loses ~$1.05–$1.45 to fees at any
   platform — fee math on a bundle of five spreads flat fees thinly.
3. **Steer repeat buyers toward the cheaper channel.** Put a Ko-fi link inside
   every PDF (allowed on Etsy if not used to bypass their checkout — check the
   current policy; the safe phrasing is "more at" a link-in-bio page rather
   than an explicit off-Etsy sales pitch).

## A 120-second decision

- Uniquely *searchable* item with broad appeal → **Etsy first.**
- Kept as part of a growing bundle for *your existing readers* → **Gumroad or
  Ko-fi first.**
- Community already gathers at your link-in-bio → **Ko-fi; upgrade to Gold
  once shop sales clear the ~$150/mo threshold.**

And the honest endgame: none of these fee schedules are what you'll compare in
year two, when your own email list (see
[Chapter 7](07-when-to-leave-marketplaces.html)) does the customer-finding and
every platform becomes a payment processor you're choosing between.
