# Product Scorecard Worksheet

Before building anything, score it here. The habit saves weeks: products that
die almost always die with a low number in one specific row, not everywhere
at once. Score each row 1–5 (5 = obviously true, 1 = obviously false), then
add.

## The eight criteria

| # | Criterion | Ask yourself | Score (1–5) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Audience fit** | Can I already reach at least a few people who want exactly this? | ____ |
| 2 | **Effort to make** | Could I finish it in an afternoon or less? | ____ |
| 3 | **Support load** | Can a buyer succeed without emailing me questions? | ____ |
| 4 | **Price ceiling** | Would a stranger pay at least $5 for this? | ____ |
| 5 | **Evergreen pull** | Will it still sell in 18+ months (or refresh cheaply)? | ____ |
| 6 | **Uniqueness** | Could a stranger tell it apart from marketplace clones? | ____ |
| 7 | **Preview-ability** | Can slide 1 of the carousel sell it in five seconds? | ____ |
| 8 | **Cousin potential** | Does it make the *next* product easier? | ____ |

**Total: ____ / 40**

## Reading the score

| Total | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| 32+ | Build it now; run the [launch checklist](launch-checklist.html) this week. |
| 25–31 | Build it, but fix the weakest row first — usually support load or preview-ability. |
| 18–24 | Park it in the backlog with a dated note about which row blocked it. |
| Under 18 | Decline kindly. Ideas are cheap; afternoons are not. |

## Where suspicion earns its keep

- **Criterion 3 is the silent killer.** Any product that will make buyers ask
  "how do I open this?" costs future-you hours you've already sold your
  afternoon away from. If three sentences can't explain the how-to page, the
  product is too complicated to sell at this scale yet.
- **Criterion 5 warns about dated products.** Seasonal bundles are fine (see
  [Chapter 6](06-seasonal-bundles.html)) — score them as evergreen only if
  the annual refresh is a real afternoon.
- **Criterion 8 is invisible at first.** The humble intro-level printables
  are what make the later family bundle work; a modest score here still
  counts for more than it looks like.
